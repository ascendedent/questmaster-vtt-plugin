# Recap registers

One session, five registers of the same Previously on paragraph. The facts are fixed. A register may add imagery of the world (cliffs, firelight, weather) but never a new action, a line of dialogue or a feeling for a character. The decisions table does not change register at all; it is a record.

## The session notes (fixed)

- Ilsabet, Brannoc and Dov escorted Master Hamm Lisker's salt caravan into the Narrows.
- Bandits blocked the road. Ilsabet promised them a quarter of the salt, payable at the far end of the pass; they let the caravan through.
- At camp, Dov searched the wagons and found crossbows hidden under the salt sacks.
- Brannoc kept one crossbow as proof and told Lisker nothing.
- The session ended when a woman walked into the firelight: the bandit chief's sister, come to collect the salt.

## Terse OSR

> Previously: the party took Hamm Lisker's salt caravan into the Narrows. Bandits blocked the road; Ilsabet bought passage with a quarter of the salt, payable at the far end. At camp, Dov searched the wagons and found crossbows under the sacks. Brannoc kept one as proof and told Lisker nothing. Now a woman stands at the edge of the firelight, sent by the bandit chief to collect.

- **Fits:** sandbox and old-school tables; groups who want the facts and want to play.
- **Opener:** "Previously:" and straight in.

## Lush literary

> When last we left them, the salt wagons were creaking into the Narrows, where the cliffs close in until the sky is a grey ribbon overhead. There the bandits stood across the road, and Ilsabet promised them a quarter of the salt at the far end of the pass, and they stepped aside. That night at camp, Dov searched the wagons and found crossbows hidden beneath the sacks. Brannoc kept one for proof and said nothing to Master Lisker. And as the fire settled, a woman stepped out of the dark beyond its light: the bandit chief's own sister, come to collect what was promised.

- **Fits:** atmospheric and mythic campaigns; tables that like to be drawn back in slowly.
- **Opener:** "When last we left them," and one image of place before the first event.
- **Note:** the cliffs and the grey ribbon of sky are world description, not new events. That is the only freedom lush takes.

## Pulp

> Last time: the Narrows! Cliffs on both sides, bandits across the road, and Ilsabet talking fast enough to buy the whole caravan through for a quarter of its salt. Then, at camp, Dov searched the wagons and found crossbows packed under the sacks! Master Lisker's salt run is not what it seems. Brannoc grabbed one crossbow as proof and kept his mouth shut. And just as the fire burned low, a stranger stepped into the light: the bandit chief's sister, here to collect!

- **Fits:** swashbuckling and action campaigns; tables that want energy before the first roll.
- **Opener:** "Last time:" and a location shouted like a title card.
- **Watch for:** "talking fast" is a manner, which pulp allows; "she shouted a threat" would be an invented action, which it does not.

## Noir

> The job was simple: walk Hamm Lisker's salt through the Narrows. Simple lasted until the bandits stood across the road, and Ilsabet bought the way through with a quarter of the salt, payable at the far end. Promises are cheap until somebody comes to collect. At camp, Dov looked under the sacks and found crossbows, which is not what salt usually looks like. Brannoc kept one and kept quiet. Then the bandit chief's sister walked into the firelight, and she wasn't there for conversation. She was there for the salt.

- **Fits:** intrigue, investigation and city campaigns; tables that enjoy a narrator with an attitude.
- **Opener:** a one-line framing of the job, then the turn.
- **Watch for:** the narrator's cynicism belongs to the narrator. "Brannoc didn't trust Lisker" would be an invented motive.

## Storybook

> Last time, three travelers walked beside a wagon train full of salt, into a narrow pass between two tall cliffs. Bandits stood in the road, but clever Ilsabet promised them a quarter of the salt if they would let the wagons by, and so they did. That night, Dov peeked under the salt sacks and found something that was not salt at all: crossbows, hidden where no one was meant to look. Brannoc kept one, to show anyone who doubted, and he did not tell Master Lisker a thing. And just then, out of the dark, came a woman who said she was the bandit chief's sister, and she had come for the salt.

- **Fits:** fairy-tale and family campaigns, and younger players.
- **Opener:** "Last time," with the party as "three travelers" before their names.

## The decisions table (the same in every register)

| Who | What they chose | What happened | What it may set in motion |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ilsabet | Promised the bandits a quarter of the salt at the far end of the pass | The bandits let the caravan through | The bandits expect payment; Lisker has not agreed to it |
| Dov | Searched the wagons at camp | Found crossbows under the salt | Lisker or his drivers may notice the sacks were moved |
| Brannoc | Kept one crossbow; told Lisker nothing | The party holds proof; Lisker does not know they know | Whoever is buying the crossbows may count them on delivery |
| The party | Did not turn back or confront Lisker | The caravan camps inside the Narrows | The sister's visit may force the question tonight |

## Behind the screen (sample)

- [unclear: whether Ilsabet promised the salt in Lisker's name or the party's]
- Threads: opened "crossbows under the salt"; advanced "the Narrows bandits".
- Follow-through: decide who is buying the crossbows; decide what the sister wants if the party refuses; Lisker's next move if he finds the missing crossbow.

## Choosing a register

| Campaign signal | Register | Opener |
|---|---|---|
| sandbox, old-school, deadly | Terse OSR | "Previously:" |
| gothic, mythic, slow-burn | Lush literary | "When last we left them," |
| swashbuckling, action, cinematic | Pulp | "Last time:" |
| intrigue, city, investigation | Noir | a one-line framing of the job |
| fairy tale, family, whimsical | Storybook | "Last time," |
