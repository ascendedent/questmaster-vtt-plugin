# Cosmic Horror: NPCs

Cosmic horror NPCs are the human face of something with no face. Each one has touched the edge, is protecting someone from it, or profits from it. Give each a want, a fear and a performable tell. The twist keeps the archetype from being the stock figure, and the best twists make the party less sure that the "right" answer is to dig.

## How to use these

- Most NPCs hold one clue each. Spread the truth so no single interview gives the whole answer.
- At least one NPC should be right, sane and ignored, and at least one should be wrong in a way that is completely reasonable.
- Store twists in the NPC's `secret`; link each NPC to the site, the faction and the thread they touch.

## Archetypes

### The scholar who knows too much
- **Twist:** lucid, correct and deliberately silent. She worked out the truth years ago and decided that telling anyone would spread it.
- **Want:** for the party to stop, and failing that, to be the one who decides what they learn.
- **Fear:** a student finding her notes.
- **Tell:** answers questions with questions, and once, without meaning to, answers a question nobody asked yet.
- Example: Magister Ilse Varrow, retired, who burns her morning's writing every evening.

### The sympathetic cultist
- **Twist:** joined to save her daughter from a fever, and it worked. The daughter is healthy and strange and happy.
- **Want:** to keep her daughter, whatever she now is.
- **Fear:** the party being right.
- **Tell:** hums the same four notes when nervous, and so does her daughter, in harmony.

### The skeptical magistrate
- **Twist:** has seen it, and is lying to keep order. He might be right to.
- **Want:** the town calm and the case closed by the end of the month.
- **Fear:** a panic, which he has seen kill more people than the thing did.
- **Tell:** writes "accidental" on every report before he has read it, then reads it carefully.

### The witness
- **Twist:** the account is perfectly accurate, but the witness is describing it from the wrong side: they were not watching it, they were part of it.
- **Want:** someone to believe them, and then to stop asking.
- **Fear:** sleeping, because they remember the other side more clearly in dreams.
- **Tell:** describes distances and directions wrongly ("it was below me, up on the hill").

### The patron
- **Twist:** wants the artifact recovered intact, not destroyed, and has a buyer.
- **Want:** a finder's fee and a seat at the table when the door opens.
- **Fear:** being left out, more than being eaten.
- **Tell:** pays generously, in advance, and asks about the item before asking about the dead.

### The changed
- **Twist:** partway transformed, and happier than they have ever been. They do not want to be saved.
- **Want:** to finish changing, at the site, on the right night.
- **Fear:** a cure.
- **Tell:** smells faintly of the sea, or of cold stone, and eats with great politeness and no appetite.
- Mercy angle: helping them finish may be kinder than forcing them back, and it adds one more servant to the thing.

### The survivor of the last investigation
- **Twist:** she won, thirty years ago. This is what winning looked like.
- **Want:** for the party to win better than she did.
- **Fear:** that her small win is the reason this is happening again.
- **Tell:** keeps a lamp lit at all times, and checks the shadows match the objects.

### The prodigy
- **Twist:** a child or apprentice who dreams the mathematics of the site, correctly, and is building something in the cellar from the dreams.
- **Want:** to finish it, because in the dreams it makes everyone stop being sad.
- **Fear:** that grown-ups will make them stop dreaming.
- **Tell:** draws the same shape on every surface and gets upset when it is wiped off.

### The archivist
- **Twist:** the archive edits itself. She keeps a second, hand-copied set so she can see what changed.
- **Want:** an outsider to confirm she is not imagining it.
- **Fear:** the day both sets agree.
- **Tell:** compares any document the party shows her against a copy before she reads it.

### The priest of a small god
- **Twist:** his mundane faith actually works, a little. His wards hold for one night and his blessings buy one hour.
- **Want:** to be useful without being noticed by anything large.
- **Fear:** being asked to do more than his god can give.
- **Tell:** blesses everything (doors, bread, the party's boots) quickly and apologetically.

### The local who keeps the custom
- **Twist:** the town's odd rituals really do hold it back, and the party's investigation is breaking them.
- **Want:** the outsiders gone before the festival.
- **Fear:** explaining, because explaining is part of what weakens it.
- **Tell:** corrects anyone who says the site's name aloud, firmly, every time.

## Performing them

- People who have touched the edge speak precisely about small things and vaguely about large ones.
- Let their bodies give clues: a too-steady hand, a habit of looking at corners, a way of avoiding mirrors.
- Never let an NPC describe the entity directly. They describe what it did, how it felt, what was missing afterward.
- Give each NPC one reason to lie that the party would respect if they knew it.
