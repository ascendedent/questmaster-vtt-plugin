# High Fantasy Epic: NPCs

Epic NPCs carry the weight of ages, so give them small, human handles. Each archetype has a twist that keeps it from the stock figure, a want, a fear and a tell the DM can perform in one gesture or line. Tie every one to a prophecy line or a place on the map.

## Rules for epic NPCs

- **Ancient does not mean solemn.** The oldest beings in the world can be petty, funny, bored or kind.
- **Nobody knows the whole prophecy.** Each NPC holds a piece, a wrong translation or a hope about it.
- **Mentors survive and are wrong.** A living mentor with a mistake to own is better drama than a scheduled death.
- **The enemy's people are people.** Give at least one of them a home, a grievance and a reason the party could understand.

## Archetypes

### The reluctant chosen
- **Twist:** refuses the prophecy and is right to; the people pushing him to accept it want a figurehead, not a hero.
- **Want:** to be a baker in the town where he grew up.
- **Fear:** that the prophecy is true and his refusal dooms everyone.
- **Tell:** picks at the birthmark everyone says is the sign.
- Example: Pell Harrowby, sixteen, who has started wearing gloves so nobody can point at his hand.

### The mentor who lives
- **Twist:** survives every danger and turns out to be wrong about something important she taught the party.
- **Want:** to undo the mistake she made the last time the bells cracked.
- **Fear:** being outgrown by her students.
- **Tell:** starts sentences with "When I was your age" and stops herself halfway.

### The last of an elder people
- **Twist:** not the last at all; their people are hiding from shame over what they did in the last age.
- **Want:** to keep the hidden city hidden.
- **Fear:** the party finding it, and judging them.
- **Tell:** speaks of their people in the past tense, then slips into the present.

### The seer who doubts
- **Twist:** honest about uncertainty; she sees prophecy as likelihood and says so, which makes the temple furious.
- **Want:** for someone to choose, so she can stop seeing every path at once.
- **Fear:** certainty, in herself or anyone else.
- **Tell:** answers questions with "most likely", and means it.

### The herald of the enemy
- **Twist:** the hero who answered the prophecy last age; what she was given for it turned her to the other side.
- **Want:** to break the cycle so no one is chosen again.
- **Fear:** that the party is as naive as she was.
- **Tell:** perfectly courteous, always offers terms before steel.

### The retired hero at the inn
- **Twist:** answered a prophecy once, and it went quietly wrong; nobody in the village knows who he was.
- **Want:** the party to succeed where he did not, without learning his name.
- **Fear:** being recognized by an old enemy, or an old friend.
- **Tell:** sharpens the kitchen knives the way soldiers sharpen swords.

### The dragon
- **Twist:** bored and curious, she trades in stories rather than gold, and keeps a ledger of the best ones.
- **Want:** an ending she has not heard before.
- **Fear:** being forgotten when the age turns.
- **Tell:** leans in and asks "and then?" at the worst possible moment.

### The exiled monarch
- **Twist:** does not want the throne back; the realm has done better without the crown, and he knows it.
- **Want:** to stay a cobbler in a border town.
- **Fear:** being dragged back as a symbol for someone else's war.
- **Tell:** calloused hands that keep fidgeting with an awl.

### The archivist
- **Twist:** has been editing the prophecy texts for decades to steer events toward the answer she believes is kindest.
- **Want:** that answer, at any cost to her reputation.
- **Fear:** an older copy surfacing.
- **Tell:** ink on her cuffs; corrects people's quotations, sometimes wrongly on purpose.

### The rival company
- **Twist:** another band of heroes with an equal claim to the prophecy, more famous and less kind, or kinder and less able.
- **Want:** to be the ones the songs remember.
- **Fear:** being second.
- **Tell:** their leader always arrives a day early and leaves a note saying so.

### The ordinary child
- **Twist:** the child who watches the party and will be the next age's hero, or the true answer to a line if the players choose it.
- **Want:** to follow them.
- **Fear:** being left behind with the grown-ups who stay home.
- **Tell:** copies one character's habit (a phrase, a way of standing).

### The enemy's lieutenant
- **Twist:** named in the prophecy too, and certain it describes him.
- **Want:** to fulfill it his way, and be welcomed home a hero.
- **Fear:** that he has misread it.
- **Tell:** quotes the line under his breath before every fight.

## Performing them fast

- One habit with an object (a pipe, a book, a bell shard), and one phrase they repeat.
- Elder beings speak in the present about things centuries gone; ordinary folk speak about the price of bread.
- Let every NPC answer the prophecy question differently when asked. Their answers are the candidate cards, in voices.
