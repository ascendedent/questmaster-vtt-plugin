# War Campaign: NPCs

War NPCs are people the war is happening to. Each archetype has a twist that keeps it from the stock figure, a want, a fear and a tell the DM can perform in one gesture or line.

## Rules for war NPCs

- **Tie every NPC to a front.** When the front moves, the NPC's life changes, and the ripple scene shows it.
- **Give recurring soldiers a small object that will outlive them** (a carved die, a sister's ribbon, a borrowed book). It becomes the human-sized consequence if they fall.
- **Meet enemies before fighting them.** A parley, a captured scout, a letter found on a body.
- **Nobody is free.** Everyone in a war answers to someone; write who, and what they would risk by disobeying.
- **NPCs leave.** Reassign, wound, capture or promote them as fronts move, and say so on-screen.

## Archetypes

### The exhausted general
- **Twist:** knows the war is lost and is secretly negotiating terms to save her soldiers, which her own court would call treason.
- **Want:** one more month of holding the line, to get the terms she needs.
- **Fear:** being remembered as the one who gave up.
- **Tell:** signs orders without reading them, then rereads every one by candlelight.
- Example: Marshal Idra Venholt, who keeps her surrender draft folded inside her prayer book.

### The quartermaster
- **Twist:** the real power in the army; whoever controls the wagons decides which regiments eat.
- **Want:** the war to end before the ledgers are audited.
- **Fear:** an honest clerk.
- **Tell:** answers every question with a number.

### The green recruit
- **Twist:** a deserter from the other side who enlisted here under a false name because this army feeds its soldiers.
- **Want:** to survive until the peace and find his sister.
- **Fear:** a prisoner who recognizes him.
- **Tell:** salutes with the wrong hand, then corrects it.

### The enemy officer with reasons
- **Twist:** invaded because the March's lords burned her valley twenty years ago, and she carries the letters that prove it.
- **Want:** the lords who did it, not the farmers.
- **Fear:** becoming the thing she is avenging.
- **Tell:** returns prisoners' personal letters unopened.

### The profiteer
- **Twist:** supplies both armies, and also funds the only field hospital that treats both sides, the one charity he has ever done.
- **Want:** the war to last exactly one more year.
- **Fear:** peace, and the hospital closing.
- **Tell:** pats his breast pocket whenever anyone mentions the hospital.

### The field surgeon
- **Twist:** keeps a list of every soldier she saved who went back to the line and died, and is deciding whether to stop saving them.
- **Want:** one season without a battle.
- **Fear:** the list getting longer than the one in her head.
- **Tell:** washes her hands mid-conversation, whether or not they need it.

### The chaplain
- **Twist:** lost his faith at the second siege and keeps performing the rites because the soldiers need them.
- **Want:** someone to confess to.
- **Fear:** a dying soldier asking him a real question about the afterlife.
- **Tell:** blesses things that do not need it: wagons, boots, the party's lunch.

### The spy in the mess
- **Twist:** reports to a third power neither army knows is watching.
- **Want:** the war to last long enough to weaken both sides.
- **Fear:** growing fond of the soldiers she reports on.
- **Tell:** always volunteers to carry the company's letters to the courier.

### The refugee matriarch
- **Twist:** negotiates passage with both armies and has become the most effective diplomat in the war.
- **Want:** a valley no army wants.
- **Fear:** her people being split up at a checkpoint.
- **Tell:** counts heads out loud at every stop.

### The hero of the last war
- **Twist:** the legend is false; the brave act was done by a soldier he left behind, who now begs in the camp.
- **Want:** to deserve the statue.
- **Fear:** the beggar.
- **Tell:** tells the famous story a little differently every time.

### The mercenary captain
- **Twist:** honors contracts absolutely, so the cheapest way to beat him is to read his contract; there is always a clause.
- **Want:** a pension for every member of the company.
- **Fear:** an employer who dies before payday.
- **Tell:** quotes clauses by number.

### The prince at the front
- **Twist:** competent, loved by the ranks, and the court wants him to die gloriously to clear the succession.
- **Want:** to win the war without being made king.
- **Fear:** his own popularity.
- **Tell:** eats with the enlisted, always last in the line.

## Performing them fast

- One phrase of camp slang and one physical burden each (a limp, a pack, a child on the hip, a cough).
- Officers speak in orders, soldiers in complaints, civilians in questions. Break the pattern when something matters.
- Name the dead. When a recurring NPC falls, the ripple scene uses their object, not a casualty count.
