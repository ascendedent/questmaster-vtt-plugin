# Political intrigue: NPC archetypes

In intrigue every NPC with a name is a node in the leverage economy. For each one, write down four things before anything else: **what they want, what they fear, what they hold over someone, and what someone holds over them.** The archetypes below are starting shapes; the twist is what keeps each from being the version the players already expect.

When drafting with `upsert_npc`, put the want, fear, tell, held leverage and owed leverage in the record, and log any debt they are owed or owe as an `upsert_thread`.

## 1. The Chancellor Who Wants Out

- **Twist:** She is the most powerful person at court and is desperate to retire, but every possible successor would wreck the realm in a season.
- **Want:** A successor she trusts. The party might become her audition.
- **Fear:** Dying in office and leaving the seat to the worst candidate.
- **Tell:** Straightens other people's papers while they talk to her.

## 2. The Spymaster With a Hollow Network

- **Twist:** Half his informants are invented. He sells polished fabrications to three factions and lives in terror that someone will check a source.
- **Want:** One real, reliable agent, so he can stop bluffing.
- **Fear:** Two of his clients comparing notes.
- **Tell:** Answers every question with a question, then writes your answer down.

## 3. The Heir Who Is Actually Good

- **Twist:** Competent, honest and kind, which is exactly the problem. Every faction backing the heir wanted a puppet and is now quietly reconsidering.
- **Want:** To rule well, without owing anyone.
- **Fear:** That there is no such thing.
- **Tell:** Asks servants their names and remembers them, which unsettles the nobles more than any threat.

## 4. The Envoy of a Fallen State

- **Twist:** Her government fell three weeks ago. Nobody here knows. She is still negotiating, now for refugees who have not yet arrived.
- **Want:** Asylum and land for her people before the news breaks.
- **Fear:** The courier who will arrive with the truth.
- **Tell:** Checks the door every time it opens, a beat too fast.

## 5. The Captain Loyal to the Office

- **Twist:** Loyal to the law, not the ruler. If the charter says the queen must be arrested, he will do it personally, with great courtesy.
- **Want:** Clean orders that do not require him to choose.
- **Fear:** Being ordered to break the law by someone with the right to order it.
- **Tell:** Quotes article and clause numbers when nervous.

## 6. The Merchant Prince in Debt

- **Twist:** His wealth is borrowed. He is the most leveraged person in any room, which makes him the most desperate and the most generous.
- **Want:** One more season of credit.
- **Fear:** A run on his name, started by a single rumour.
- **Tell:** Pays for everything, loudly, and never in coin.

## 7. The Disgraced Former Favourite

- **Twist:** Exiled to a country house ten years ago, she knows every secret of the old court. Half are now harmless. The other half could topple the current one, and she does not know which half is which.
- **Want:** Rehabilitation, or revenge if rehabilitation is refused.
- **Fear:** Being forgotten more than being punished.
- **Tell:** Refers to current officials by the jobs they held a decade ago.

## 8. The Reformer Who Is Right

- **Twist:** He is entirely correct about the corruption, and his remedy is a purge.
- **Want:** A clean council, by any means.
- **Fear:** That he will compromise once and become what he hates.
- **Tell:** Refuses food and drink at every meeting, as if it might be bribery.

## 9. The Salon Host

- **Twist:** She trades no secrets at all. She trades introductions, and she keeps the guest list, which is the most dangerous document in the city.
- **Want:** To remain the one house every faction must visit.
- **Fear:** Open war, which empties salons.
- **Tell:** Never sits down at her own gatherings.

## 10. The Clerk Who Sets the Agenda

- **Twist:** The quietest person on the council is the most powerful, because she decides what gets discussed and in what order. Items she dislikes are scheduled after the wine.
- **Want:** An orderly council that does not need her to rig it.
- **Fear:** A crisis big enough to bypass her agenda.
- **Tell:** Ink-stained cuff on the left hand only; she writes with both.

## 11. The Hostage Ward

- **Twist:** A rival house's child, raised at court as surety. Now an adult and loyal to their captors. Their birth family is planning a rescue they do not want.
- **Want:** To be asked, for once, where they belong.
- **Fear:** Being traded back like a returned loan.
- **Tell:** Uses the court's turns of phrase with the precision of a convert.

## 12. The Old Enemy Offering Help

- **Twist:** The offer is sincere. A worse threat has frightened them into honesty, and nobody will believe it, including the players.
- **Want:** An alliance of convenience before it is too late.
- **Fear:** That the party's suspicion will waste the time they have.
- **Tell:** Arrives alone and unarmed, which is a calculated message in itself.

## Playing them at the table

- **Every NPC speaks in positions, thinks in interests.** Let them demand the charter; let the players discover they need money.
- **Show leverage before using it.** An NPC who will later threaten exposure first mentions, lightly, a dinner the party would rather forget.
- **Give each a line they will not cross.** It makes them people, and it is a pressure point.
- **Let the powerful be busy.** A short audience with a duke is a reward; an NPC who always has time is not powerful.
- **Two new names per session is plenty.** Reuse known NPCs in new contexts before adding more.
