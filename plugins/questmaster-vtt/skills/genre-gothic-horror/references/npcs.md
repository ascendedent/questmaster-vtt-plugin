# Gothic Horror: NPCs

Every gothic NPC is tied to the house, the family or the curse. Give each one a want, a fear and a tell the DM can perform in a single gesture or line. The twist is what keeps them from being the stock figure; never let the twist be "secretly evil" alone.

## How to use these

- Pick 4 to 6 for a household. More than that and the players stop tracking who lied.
- At least one must be genuinely kind, and at least one kindness must be the most dangerous thing in the house.
- Store the twist in the NPC's `secret`, the tell in their notes, and link every NPC to the family faction and the house location.

## Archetypes

### The devoted housekeeper
- **Twist:** loyal to the house, not the family. She would let any heir die to keep the roof standing, and has.
- **Want:** the house to outlive everyone in it.
- **Fear:** being dismissed and dying somewhere that does not know her.
- **Tell:** straightens whatever the party has touched, without looking at it, mid-sentence.
- Example: Mother Tamsel, forty-one years in service, keys on a cord she never takes off, even to sleep.

### The charming heir
- **Twist:** already knows about the curse and is trying to break it, by a method worse than the curse (moving it onto strangers, starting with the party).
- **Want:** to be the generation that got free.
- **Fear:** that he is exactly like his grandfather.
- **Tell:** asks the party personal questions with real interest and remembers every answer.
- Example: Corwin Ashlade, who keeps a guest book and has quietly underlined some of the names.

### The invalid in the east wing
- **Twist:** not ill. Hiding from the family, faking sickness for years, and the only person in the house who has told nobody a lie.
- **Want:** a way out that does not cost the servant who brings her meals.
- **Fear:** the stairs, because the last person who carried her down them was sold.
- **Tell:** speaks only in whispers, then says one sentence at full voice when she means it.

### The village priest
- **Twist:** the church was built with the family's money, and he keeps the ledger of who went into the curse. He believes he is keeping a roll of martyrs.
- **Want:** absolution he cannot grant himself.
- **Fear:** the bishop's visit, due next month.
- **Tell:** touches the book in his coat pocket whenever the family is named.

### The ghost who asks for help
- **Twist:** wants help hurting someone, and that someone mostly deserves it.
- **Want:** the living descendant of her murderer to know what was done.
- **Fear:** being forgotten, which for her is a second death she can feel coming.
- **Tell:** the temperature drops only on the side of the room where the guilty person stands.
- Mercy angle: laying her to rest requires a confession from a living person who will be ruined by it.

### The patriarch who cannot die
- **Twist:** the monster is begging to be destroyed, and the family will not allow it, because his existence pays for theirs.
- **Want:** to stop.
- **Fear:** that he will start to enjoy it again.
- **Tell:** apologizes before every cruelty, sincerely.

### The physician
- **Twist:** the cure works. The cost is paid by someone else, a patient she never names, in the room below the clinic.
- **Want:** to be thanked by the family that looks down on her.
- **Fear:** a case she cannot cure, because then she would have to stop.
- **Tell:** washes her hands before shaking anyone's, and again after.

### The child who sees
- **Twist:** is lying about what she sees, deliberately, to steer the adults away from the person she is protecting.
- **Want:** for her brother to stay in the walls where he is safe.
- **Fear:** being sent to school in the city.
- **Tell:** describes ghosts with impossible detail and gets the colors wrong every time.

### The newcomer governess
- **Twist:** an heir from a disowned branch of the family, here to claim what the will owes her mother, and the curse comes with the claim.
- **Want:** proof of her birth, kept in the family chapel.
- **Fear:** that the family will recognize her face from the portraits before she has the proof.
- **Tell:** knows where things are in the house before she is shown.

### The reformed hunter
- **Twist:** a previous victim of the curse, cured years ago, now hunting the afflicted with a convert's zeal and no mercy for the innocent ones.
- **Want:** to kill the thing that made him, so he can stop being grateful it let him go.
- **Fear:** the itch in his scars on certain nights.
- **Tell:** offers the party his flask, and it is holy water, always.

### The portrait painter
- **Twist:** paints what she sees, and she sees what people will become. The family keeps commissioning portraits and burning them.
- **Want:** to finish one honest portrait the family will let hang.
- **Fear:** painting herself.
- **Tell:** stares at one player for slightly too long, then picks up charcoal.

### The faithful fiance
- **Twist:** knows exactly what family he is marrying into and is doing it to get inside and burn it down.
- **Want:** justice for a sister the family "rooted" a generation ago.
- **Fear:** that he will fall in love with his bride, who is innocent.
- **Tell:** proposes toasts that are a little too pointed, then laughs them off.

## Performing them

- Give each NPC one physical habit and one phrase. The habit is for nervous scenes, the phrase is for when they lie.
- Gothic NPCs speak politely while saying terrible things. Let the horror live in the gap between the words and the content.
- Never let an NPC explain the curse in full. Each knows one piece, and wants to keep it for a reason the party can discover.
