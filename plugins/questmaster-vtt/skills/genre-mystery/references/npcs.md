# Mystery: NPC archetypes

Every NPC in a mystery is a scene the players can visit. For each one, write **what they know, what they will say, what they hide, and why**, plus a want, a fear and a performable tell. Draft them with `upsert_npc`; keep what they hide and why in the DM-only notes, and stage what they reveal with `stage_secret` aimed at the character who asks the right question.

## 1. The Obvious Suspect

- **Twist:** Guilty of something else. Their lie is the red herring, and it is true about a different secret.
- **Want:** To keep that other secret buried.
- **Fear:** That the investigators find it and assume the worst.
- **Tell:** Over-answers simple questions, under-answers the one about last night.
- **Use:** Following them reveals a real secret and usually one real clue about the crime.

## 2. The Witness Protecting Someone

- **Twist:** Lies to shield a person they love, whom they wrongly believe is guilty.
- **Want:** That person safe.
- **Fear:** Being the one whose testimony convicts them.
- **Tell:** Glances toward the same door or window whenever the loved one is mentioned.
- **Use:** Prove the loved one innocent and the witness tells the truth at once.

## 3. The Culprit Making Amends

- **Twist:** The culprit is quietly trying to repair the harm: paying the victim's family anonymously, finishing the victim's work. The amends are themselves a clue.
- **Want:** To be forgiven without confessing.
- **Fear:** Being seen, more than being punished.
- **Tell:** Speaks of the victim in the present tense, then corrects themselves too quickly.

## 4. The Victim, Through What Remains

- **Twist:** Not who the town thought. The victim's own secret is the motive.
- **Want (in life):** Something the players can reconstruct from their rooms, letters and habits.
- **Fear (in life):** Whatever their locked drawer is about.
- **Tell:** A habit others mention ("always walked the long way home") that turns out to be meaningful.

## 5. The Rival Investigator

- **Twist:** Competent and right about half of it. Will trade clues with the party, fairly, and race them for the other half.
- **Want:** The credit, or the bounty.
- **Fear:** Being wrong in public.
- **Tell:** Writes in a small notebook and turns it face down when anyone approaches.

## 6. The Overworked Expert

- **Twist:** A real expert (a coroner, an alchemist, a sage) who made one mistake on this case from exhaustion and will defend it until shown proof.
- **Want:** Sleep, and to be trusted.
- **Fear:** That the mistake becomes known.
- **Tell:** Cites the number of bodies, samples or books they have handled this month.

## 7. The Stale Gossip

- **Twist:** Everything they say is true, but three weeks out of date. The difference between then and now is a clue.
- **Want:** Company, and to be the first to know.
- **Fear:** Being left out.
- **Tell:** Begins every sentence with "Now, you didn't hear this from me".

## 8. The Authority Who Wants It Closed

- **Twist:** Not corrupt. Afraid that the truth will start a riot, a feud or a war, and honestly believes a quiet lie is kinder.
- **Want:** Peace in the town.
- **Fear:** What happens if the truth is spoken aloud.
- **Tell:** Agrees with everything the players say, then does nothing.

## 9. The Unknowing Accomplice

- **Twist:** Helped with what they thought was a small crime (moving a cart, lending a key). Has not realised it was part of a murder.
- **Want:** To stay out of trouble.
- **Fear:** Learning what they helped do.
- **Tell:** Rubs the hand that did the deed.
- **Use:** Shown the truth, they break and tell everything.

## 10. The One Who Knows But Cannot Say

- **Twist:** Bound by an oath, a confessional seal, a contract or a geas. Can only answer in gestures, yes or no, or by pointing the players to a place.
- **Want:** For the players to ask the right yes-or-no question.
- **Fear:** Breaking the oath and losing what it protects.
- **Tell:** Long silences, then a very precise word.

## 11. The Next Victim

- **Twist:** Knows they are next and why. Will not say, because the reason is shameful (they took part in the original wrong).
- **Want:** To survive without confessing.
- **Fear:** Both the culprit and the truth.
- **Tell:** Has moved their bed, changed their route, hired a guard they cannot afford.

## 12. The Overlooked Servant

- **Twist:** Noticed everything. Nobody asked, because nobody sees them.
- **Want:** To be asked, by name, with respect.
- **Fear:** Being dismissed for speaking about their employers.
- **Tell:** Corrects small factual errors under their breath.

## Interviewing them at the table

- **Every NPC volunteers one thing.** Even the hostile ones. It keeps conversations moving.
- **A good question earns a clue, a good roll earns a tell.** Let the dice reveal nervousness; let the players' questions reveal facts.
- **Lies have reasons.** If an NPC lies, the reason is either the crime or a red herring. Know which.
- **Let NPCs ask back.** A suspect who asks "why do you want to know?" forces the players to show their hand.
